## This Agreement

This agreement outlines the local SEO services KangoMedia Web Design ("we," "us," or "our") will provide to the Client identified above, the investment required, and the responsibilities of both parties. It is intended to be straightforward and fair to everyone involved.

The Local Visibility Plan is a month-to-month local SEO engagement covering the core areas that drive organic search performance: Google Business Profile management, technical and on-page optimization, content creation, and authority building.

---

## What We'll Do

### Google Business Profile

Your Google Business Profile controls how your business appears in Google Maps and local search results. An incomplete or inactive profile limits your visibility to nearby customers. We optimize and maintain your profile, keep it active with regular posts and Q&A, and manage your review presence.

| Deliverable | Details |
|---|---|
| GBP Audit & Optimization | Full audit and optimization of GBP including business info, categories, service areas, hours, photos, and services |
| GBP Posts | 2 posts per month, scheduled and published on Client's behalf |
| GBP Q&A | 2 question-and-answer entries per month added to Client's GBP listing |
| GBP Review Response | Professional, on-brand responses to all incoming reviews, posted within 24 hours of the review going live. |

### Technical & On-Page SEO

Technical issues — broken links, missing metadata, incorrect canonical tags — silently prevent Google from properly crawling and ranking your pages. We audit your site, resolve what we find, and monitor for new issues so they are addressed before they affect performance.

| Deliverable | Details |
|---|---|
| Metadata Optimization | Title tags, meta descriptions, and H1 headers optimized across money pages and updated on all new content. |
| Alt Image Text | Descriptive alt text applied to all existing and new images. |
| Structured Data (Schema) | Schema markup added to your business info, services, FAQs, and articles — the structured data Google uses to power rich search results like FAQ accordions, review stars, and business cards. |
| Internal Linking | Link structure improved to direct authority toward highest-value pages, updated as new content is published. |
| Canonical Tags & Redirects | Missing or incorrect canonical tags corrected; outdated redirects updated. |

### Content

Search engines determine topical authority based on the depth and relevance of your content. Consistent blog content targeting the right keywords expands the range of searches your site appears in and reinforces your core service pages.

| Deliverable | Details |
|---|---|
| Blog Writing | 2 blog posts per month (~1,500 words each), targeting keywords relevant to Client's services and local market. All posts submitted for Client approval before publishing. |
| Topical Depth Enhancements | Related concepts and supporting terms added to your core pages and all new content. When Google sees a page covers a topic *thoroughly* — not just the main keyword but the surrounding network of related ideas — that page ranks higher for the topic overall. |

### Authority Building

Rankings depend not only on what is on your website but on how the broader internet references your business. Citations ensure your business information is accurate and consistent across directories, and press releases generate backlinks from news outlets — both of which signal credibility to search engines.

| Deliverable | Details |
|---|---|
| Local Citations | Business information submitted to approximately 25 local directories, with ongoing monitoring for accuracy and consistency. |
| Press Release Distribution | 1 press release per month, written and distributed through a national press release wire. Each release is picked up across a network of news, industry, and regional outlets. Every pickup creates an authoritative backlink to your site — one of the strongest trust signals in Google's ranking system. Coverage volume varies by month and topic, but the cumulative effect is a steady stream of credibility signals that lift the rest of your SEO over time. |

## What You Can Expect

SEO results are cumulative and build over time. The following is a general timeline based on consistent execution of services. Actual results vary based on market competition, website quality, industry, and other factors outside our control.

- **Month 1:** GBP fully optimized, technical foundation addressed, citations submitted, first content live.
- **Months 2–3:** Blog content begins indexing. Visibility increases for longer-tail search terms. Initial ranking improvements on targeted pages.
- **Months 4–6:** Consistent ranking improvements across primary service pages. Increase in organic traffic and inbound inquiries.
- **Month 6+:** Sustained local search presence with compounding gains from content and authority building.
