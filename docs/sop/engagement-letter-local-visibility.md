# Engagement Letter Template — Local Visibility Plan

> **⚠️ This is a template, not legal advice.** Before sending the first signed version, have a Colorado business attorney review it for your specific situation. Once you've got an attorney-reviewed v1, future engagements can use the same base and you only need to update the `{{PLACEHOLDERS}}` and any service-specific clauses. Notes in `> blockquotes` like this one are guidance for you, Freddy — strip them out before sending to a client.

> **How to use this template:**
> 1. Copy the body below into a fresh document (Google Doc, PDF in a contract tool, or your e-sign platform of choice).
> 2. Find/replace every `{{PLACEHOLDER}}` (there are about 12).
> 3. Strip out every blockquote that starts with `> **NOTE:**` — those are your internal cues, not for the client.
> 4. Send via an e-signature tool (Dropbox Sign, DocuSign, Adobe Sign) with the first invoice attached or linked.
> 5. **Don't start work until the engagement letter is signed AND the first invoice is paid.** This is a hard rule. The Day-1 baseline post in the platform is the first thing they see *after* both happen.

### Quick summary (the deal in 30 seconds)

- **What you get:** Monthly local SEO and content services per the scope below.
- **What it costs:** **${{MONTHLY_FEE}}/month**, billed on the **{{BILLING_DAY}}** of each month, in advance.
- **Initial term:** **6 months**, then continues month-to-month until either party gives 30 days written notice.
- **What you do:** Approve content drafts within 5 business days. Provide platform access (Google Business Profile, Google Analytics, Google Search Console). Trust the process for at least the first 60 days.
- **What we don't promise:** Specific rankings, specific keyword positions, or guaranteed leads. (Anyone who promises these things is lying.)

If anything in the body below conflicts with this summary, the **body controls.**

### 1. Scope of services

Each calendar month during the term, KangoMedia will deliver the following to Client under the **Local Visibility Plan**:

1. **Two (2) blog posts** — original, SEO-optimized articles (1,200–2,000 words each) on topics agreed in the monthly content plan. Drafted by KangoMedia, reviewed by Client, and published to Client's website by KangoMedia.
2. **Two (2) Google Business Profile posts** — short-form updates posted to Client's Google Business Profile.
3. **Two (2) Google Business Profile Q&As** — drafted Q&A entries posted to Client's Google Business Profile.
4. **One (1) press release** — drafted by KangoMedia and distributed via a third-party press release distribution network. Distribution does not guarantee pickup by any specific publication.
5. **Twenty-five (25) directory and citation listings** — Client's business listed on a curated set of directories selected by KangoMedia. Some directories may take 2–6 weeks to publish or verify; KangoMedia will deliver submissions, not guaranteed live dates per directory.
6. **Ongoing Google Business Profile optimization** — review and update of Client's GBP categories, services, photos, hours, and Q&A surface throughout each month.
7. **Monthly performance report** — a written report delivered through the KangoMedia client portal at month-end summarizing the work delivered and performance metrics for the month.
8. **Access to the KangoMedia client portal** — for content review, deliverable visibility, and report access.

### 2. Services NOT included

For clarity, the Local Visibility Plan **does not** include any of the following. These can be added under a separate engagement at additional cost:

- Google Ads, Microsoft Ads, or any paid advertising campaign management
- Website redesign, redevelopment, or hosting
- Email marketing or marketing automation
- Social media management (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, X, etc.)
- E-commerce SEO or product feed optimization
- Legal review of content (Client is responsible for ensuring published content complies with any industry-specific regulations applicable to its business)
- Negative review removal (KangoMedia will help Client respond to reviews, but cannot remove reviews from Google, Yelp, or any other platform)
- Any work that violates the Terms of Service of Google, Yelp, or any other platform, or that violates applicable law

> **NOTE:** keep this list current. Every time a prospect asks "do you do X?" and the answer is no, add X here.

### 3. Term and renewal

The **initial term** is **six (6) months** beginning on the Effective Date.

After the initial term, this engagement automatically continues **on a month-to-month basis** until either party terminates it under Section 8 below.

> **NOTE:** the 6-month initial commitment is what makes the SEO math work. The first 60 days are setup costs (baseline, audits, GBP cleanup, directory submissions) that don't produce visible results. Pulling out at month 3 means Client paid for setup and walked away before the compounding kicked in. Hold the line on 6 months — if a prospect can't commit, decline and offer a one-time engagement instead.

### 4. Fees and payment

The monthly fee for the Local Visibility Plan is **${{MONTHLY_FEE}} USD per month**, exclusive of any applicable sales tax.

- **Billing date:** The {{BILLING_DAY}} of each month. The first invoice is due on or before the Effective Date and must be paid before work begins.
- **Payment method:** {{PAYMENT_METHODS}} (e.g., ACH, credit card via {{PAYMENT_PROCESSOR}}, or check).
- **Late payment:** Invoices are due **on receipt**. Invoices unpaid after **7 days** will pause all active work, including content publication, until paid. Invoices unpaid after **30 days** are grounds for termination under Section 8(c) without refund of any partial work delivered.
- **No refunds for partial months.** Work is delivered on a monthly cadence; mid-month cancellations do not pro-rate.

### 5. Client responsibilities

For KangoMedia to deliver, Client agrees to:

1. **Provide platform access** within 5 business days of the Effective Date:
   - Manager-level access to Client's Google Business Profile (Client retains ownership)
   - Read/view access to Google Analytics 4 (Client's primary property)
   - Owner access to Google Search Console for the property KangoMedia is publishing to
   - Editor or contributor access to Client's website CMS for the purpose of publishing approved content
2. **Review and approve content drafts within 5 business days** of receipt through the KangoMedia client portal. **Drafts not reviewed within 5 business days will be deemed approved** and published as drafted. (This isn't punitive — it exists because Client's slow approvals are the #1 reason agencies underperform, and Client is paying us to be timely.)
3. **Designate one (1) primary contact** authorized to approve content, respond to questions, and provide access. The primary contact is:
   - **Name:** {{CLIENT_CONTACT_NAME}}
   - **Email:** {{CLIENT_CONTACT_EMAIL}}
   - **Phone:** {{CLIENT_CONTACT_PHONE}}
4. **Cooperate in responding to reviews** Client receives on Google, Yelp, or other platforms — KangoMedia will draft suggested responses and Client retains final approval and posting authority over its own customer-facing replies.
5. **Provide accurate and lawful information** about the business for use in content, listings, and press releases. KangoMedia is not responsible for factual errors that originate from information Client provides.
6. **Not engage another SEO provider** for the same domain during the term without notifying KangoMedia. Conflicting SEO work can undo each other's efforts and make performance impossible to attribute.

### 6. Content ownership and license

- **Content published to Client's website**, Client's Google Business Profile, and Client's directory listings: Client owns the published content outright as a work-for-hire upon full payment of the month in which it was published.
- **KangoMedia's tools, templates, prompts, software, and methodologies** (including the KangoMedia platform and any AI-generated content prompts, scoring algorithms, or strategic frameworks): remain the exclusive property of KangoMedia. Client receives no license to reuse these.
- **Press release attribution:** Press releases distributed by KangoMedia on Client's behalf may be attributed to KangoMedia in the byline or contact line as the publishing agency. Client may request a different attribution structure in writing.
- **Portfolio rights:** KangoMedia may reference the engagement (Client's name and a high-level description of the work) in its case studies and marketing materials, unless Client objects in writing. KangoMedia will not disclose Client's confidential business information (revenue, customer lists, internal strategy) in any portfolio use without Client's prior written consent.

### 7. Confidentiality

Each party agrees to treat as confidential any non-public business information shared by the other party (financials, customer data, internal strategy, login credentials, etc.) and to use it only for the purpose of performing this engagement. This obligation survives termination of this engagement for a period of two (2) years.

### 8. Termination

This engagement may be terminated as follows:

a. **For convenience by Client after the initial term**, on **30 days written notice** to KangoMedia. The 30-day notice period must be paid in full and KangoMedia will continue to deliver services through the notice period.

b. **For convenience by KangoMedia**, on **30 days written notice** to Client at any time. KangoMedia will continue to deliver services through the notice period.

c. **For cause by either party**, immediately upon written notice, if the other party materially breaches this engagement and fails to cure the breach within 10 business days of receiving written notice describing the breach. Non-payment beyond 30 days, repeated failure to provide required access, and any request to perform services that violate platform terms of service or applicable law are deemed material breaches.

d. **Effect of termination:** Upon termination, KangoMedia will (i) complete any work for the current paid month, (ii) deliver to Client copies of any drafts in progress, (iii) revoke its access to Client's accounts, and (iv) invoice for any unpaid amounts. Client retains all content already published. Client does not receive a refund for any partial month.

### 9. No guarantee of results

Client acknowledges that:

- **Search engine rankings, traffic volumes, conversion rates, and revenue outcomes depend on factors outside KangoMedia's control**, including but not limited to Google's algorithm changes, competitor activity, Client's website performance and conversion experience, Client's pricing and offer, seasonality, and Client's responsiveness to inbound leads.
- **KangoMedia makes no guarantee, express or implied, of any specific ranking, traffic level, lead volume, or revenue outcome.**
- **KangoMedia's commitment is to deliver the deliverables in Section 1 each month** and to do so using methods that comply with Google's published webmaster guidelines and applicable law.
- **SEO is a long-horizon investment.** Client acknowledges that meaningful results typically materialize between months 4–6 of consistent execution, and that the first 60 days are primarily setup work that does not produce visible ranking or traffic changes.

### 10. Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law:

- **KangoMedia's total liability** under this engagement, regardless of the cause of action, is capped at the **total fees paid by Client to KangoMedia in the three (3) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim.**
- **Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, special, or punitive damages**, including lost profits, lost revenue, or loss of goodwill.
- These limits do not apply to (a) Client's payment obligations, (b) breach of confidentiality, or (c) willful misconduct or fraud.

### 11. Communication and notices

- **Day-to-day communication** happens by email between the parties' primary contacts and through the KangoMedia client portal.
- **Formal notices** required under this engagement (termination, breach, etc.) must be sent by email with delivery confirmation and a copy by certified mail to the addresses listed at the top of this letter. Notice is effective on receipt of the email if delivery is confirmed; otherwise on receipt of the certified mail.
- **Response time expectation:** KangoMedia will respond to non-urgent Client emails within one (1) business day. Drafts published through the portal trigger an email notification; Client's 5-business-day review clock starts from that notification.

### 12. Independent contractor

KangoMedia performs these services as an independent contractor. Nothing in this engagement creates an employer-employee, partnership, joint venture, or agency relationship beyond the limited authority KangoMedia exercises in publishing content and listings on Client's behalf with Client's approval.
